Certification Requirements

To earn the SpatialEdge Solutions – ArcGIS Pro Advanced Professional Certification, a participant must:

  • Attend at least 80% of the training sessions.

  • Score 50% or above overall (minimum 25/50).

  • Complete the practical examination.

  • Submit all required project outputs.

For your SpatialEdge Solutions Certification, I recommend using a transparent grading system that students and employers can easily understand.

Marks Distribution

Examination Maximum Marks
MCQ Examination 25

Total 50

Step 1: Calculate Total Marks

Formula

Total Marks = MCQ Marks + Practical Marks

Example

MCQ = 18

Practical = 22

Total = 18 + 22 = 40

Step 2: Calculate Percentage

The percentage is calculated as:

Percentage = (Total Marks ÷ 50) × 100

Example

Percentage = (40 ÷ 50) × 100

= 80%

Certification Eligibility

A candidate will be declared PASS only if all of the following conditions are met:

  • Overall Score: At least 25 out of 50 (50%)

  • MCQ Score: At least 10 out of 25 (40%)

  • Practical Score: At least 15 out of 25 (60%)

This ensures that candidates cannot pass solely by performing well in theory while lacking practical GIS skills.
