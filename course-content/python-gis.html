Pass Criteria

A candidate must satisfy all of the following conditions:

Requirement Minimum Marks
MCQ Examination 8 / 20 (40%)
Practical Examination 12 / 20 (60%)
Overall Total 20 / 40 (50%)

Note: If a candidate fails to achieve the minimum marks in either the MCQ or Practical examination, the overall result will be FAIL, even if the total marks are 20 or above.

Percentage Calculation

Use the following formula:

$$Percentage = ( Total Marks Obtained / 40 ) × 100 $$

Example

  • MCQ = 17

  • Practical = 18

  • Total = 35

Percentage

= (35 ÷ 40) × 100

= 87.50%
